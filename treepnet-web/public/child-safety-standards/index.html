Child Safety Standards

Last updated: 6 August 2026

TreepNet is committed to protecting children and has zero tolerance for child sexual abuse and exploitation (CSAE) on our platform. This page sets out the standards we follow to keep children safe, in line with Google Play’s Child Safety Standards policy and applicable laws.

1. Prohibited content and conduct

We strictly prohibit, and remove, any content or behaviour that sexually exploits or endangers children, including but not limited to:

  • Child sexual abuse material (CSAM) in any form.
  • Grooming, solicitation, sextortion, or enticement of a minor.
  • Sexualisation of minors, or trafficking of children.
  • Sharing, promoting, or linking to any of the above.

2. Reporting

Users can report harmful content or accounts directly inside the app using the in-app “Report” option available on posts, stories, messages, and profiles. Reports are reviewed by our moderation team. You can also contact us at treepnetofficial@gmail.com.

3. Our response

When we become aware of CSAE, we act promptly to:

  • Remove the content and disable the offending account(s).
  • Preserve relevant evidence as required by law.
  • Report confirmed CSAM to the National Center for Missing & Exploited Children (NCMEC) and/or the relevant national and regional authorities.
  • Cooperate with law enforcement in accordance with applicable law.

4. Prevention

We enforce a minimum age requirement, apply content moderation, and act on user reports to prevent misuse of the service by or against minors.

5. Legal compliance

TreepNet complies with all applicable child-safety laws and provides mechanisms for users to report CSAE to national and regional authorities.

6. Contact

Child-safety point of contact: treepnetofficial@gmail.com.
