Privacy Policy

Last updated: August 4, 2026

This Privacy Policy explains what information the Treepnet mobile application (“Treepnet”, “the app”, “we”, “us”) collects, how we use it, and the choices you have. Treepnet is a travel-based social network where the places you post from light up your personal world map.

1. Information we collect

We collect only what the app needs to work:

  • Account information — the email address, username and display name you provide when you create an account. Sign-in and account security are handled by Microsoft Entra External ID.
  • Profile information — anything you add to your profile, such as an avatar photo, bio, an optional website/social link, date of birth and gender.
  • Content you create — photos you post, stories, captions, comments, likes, saved posts and the regions/places associated with your posts (used to build your travel map). This content is stored on our servers and, for public accounts, is visible to other users.
  • Location data — with your permission, we use your device’s approximate or precise location to tag a story or post with the place it was taken and to colour the regions on your travel map. We also read the GPS tag embedded in a photo you choose, when present. Location is used only while you are using the app; Treepnet does not track your location in the background.
  • Camera & photos — with your permission, we access the camera to take pictures and your photo library so you can select existing images to share. Nothing is uploaded until you choose to post it.
  • Direct messages — messages you send to other users are stored so they can be delivered and shown in your conversations.
  • Referrals — if you invite friends with your invite link, we record how many people registered through your link so we can grant the associated rewards.
  • Usage & device analytics — we use PostHog to collect basic, aggregated analytics (for example app opens, screens viewed, app version, device model, operating system and language) to understand how the app is used and improve it.

Treepnet does not collect microphone/audio, contacts, SMS, call logs, or precise advertising identifiers, and does not require access to all files on your device.

2. How we use your information

  • To create and secure your account and sign you in.
  • To provide the core features — posting, stories, the travel map, comments, likes, saved posts and direct messaging.
  • To place your posts and stories on the map by region/place.
  • To operate the referral program and grant rewards.
  • To maintain, troubleshoot and improve the app.

3. Device permissions

The app asks for these permissions using your device’s standard prompts, and only uses them for the purpose described. You can grant or revoke each one at any time in your device settings.

  • Camera — to take photos for posts and stories.
  • Photos / media images — to select existing photos to share.
  • Location — to tag content with the place it was taken (foreground only).

4. How your information is shared

We do not sell your personal information. We share data only as needed to run the service:

  • Other users — content you post from a public account (posts, stories, profile, comments, likes) is visible to others. Direct messages are visible to the people in the conversation.
  • Service providers — we use Microsoft Azure (hosting, database and media storage), Microsoft Entra External ID (authentication) and PostHog (analytics). These providers process data on our behalf under their own security and privacy terms.
  • Legal reasons — if required by law, or to protect the rights, safety and security of our users and the service.

5. Where data is stored & security

Your data is stored on Microsoft Azure infrastructure (European region). Data is encrypted in transit using HTTPS/TLS. While no method of transmission or storage is completely secure, we take reasonable measures to protect your information.

6. Data retention & deletion

We keep your information for as long as your account is active. You can delete your account at any time from Settings → Delete account inside the app; this removes your account and associated content from our systems. You may also contact us to request deletion.

7. Children

Treepnet is not intended for children under 13 (or the minimum age required in your country). We do not knowingly collect personal information from children. If you believe a child has provided us with personal information, please contact us and we will delete it.

8. Your rights & choices

  • Access and update your profile information in the app.
  • Control camera, photos and location access from your device settings.
  • Make your account private so your content is limited to approved followers.
  • Delete your account and data at any time.

9. Changes to this policy

We may update this Privacy Policy from time to time. When we do, we will revise the “Last updated” date above. Significant changes may also be announced in the app.
